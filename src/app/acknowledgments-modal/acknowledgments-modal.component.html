Acknowledgments

This project was made possible by a Follow-On grant from the Arts and Humanities Research Council (AHRC), running from March to July 2025 ('Humours of an Election: Annotation, Animation, Education', AH/Z507556/1). It developed from an earlier AHRC grant, 'Eighteenth-Century Political Participation and Electoral Culture' (ECPPEC) that ran from 2020 to 2023, the main output of which can be found at https://ecppec.ncl.ac.uk/.

Both projects were led by Professor Matthew Grenby (Newcastle University), with co-investigators Professor Elaine Chalus (University of Liverpool) and Dr. Tom Schofield (Newcastle). Dr Kendra Packham worked on the project as Senior Research and Innovation Associate, and Dan Foster-Smith as Research Software Developer (both Newcastle). Chalus, Grenby and Packham wrote the annotations for the paintings, Grenby and Packham wrote the scripts for the animations, and Foster-Smith and Schofield developed the online platform, AI-generated animations and interactive functions.

The project was developed in partnership with the Sir John Soane Museum, with particular support from Jo Tinworth, Collections Curator (who has written extensive catalogue entries for the Soane's Hogarth paintings); Rhiannon Litterick, Public Programmes & Young People Manager; and Tallulah Smart, Museum Learning Officer.

New, high-resolution digital images of William Hogarth's four 'Humours of an Election' paintings were produced as part of the project. We are grateful for help with this photography, and for permission to use the images, from the Sir John Soane Museum which has held the paintings since they were purchased by Soane in 1823.

This resource was co-designed through a series of workshops with young people. We are grateful for the contributions made by Key Stage 3 students and their teachers at East Barnet School, London (in particular Mr Elliot Richman) and St Benet Biscop Catholic Academy, Bedlington (facilitated by the Education Outreach officers at Newcastle University Library), and by members of the Youth Panel at the Sir John Soane Museum.

Music for the project was arranged and performed by Nancy Kerr (violin, viola, viola da gamba, voice) and Aisling Holmes (border pipes), Matt Quinn provided vocals; the music was recorded at Powered Flight Music. Kendra Packham selected, abridged and was historical adviser on the musical pieces. Voices for the animations were provided by Bertie Fenton, Kerry Gooderson, Katie Guicciardi, Nancy Kerr, Daniel Lille and Cameron Medford-Hawkins.

For enquiries about the project please contact the Project Lead, Matthew Grenby.
